About techpay

techpay is a small publishing project that makes short videos about software tools people can use to earn or save money. Each video covers one tool, tested before it goes out, and this page keeps the real link to every tool we have shown.

The videos are produced by the account owner and uploaded to the @techpay__ TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. The app only posts to that one account. It does not read viewer data, messages or activity.

Questions or a tool you want covered? Message @techpay__ on TikTok.
