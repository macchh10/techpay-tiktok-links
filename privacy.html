Privacy policy

@techpay__ · last updated September 22, 2026

@techpay__ publishes short videos about software tools. The videos are made and posted by an automated system run by the account owner. This page explains what that system handles.

What we collect

Nothing from viewers. The system does not read your profile, your messages or your activity. It holds one thing: an access token for the @techpay__ account itself, granted by its owner, used only to upload videos to that account.

What we do not do

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers. We run no analytics on this site and set no cookies. Comments and likes stay on TikTok under TikTok's own policies.

The tools we link to

Links on this site point to third-party projects, mostly on GitHub. Their own policies apply once you leave. We have no relationship with them and receive nothing for linking.

Deleting data

The account owner can revoke the system's access at any time in TikTok settings, which deletes the token. For anything else, message @techpay__ on TikTok.

Contact

Message @techpay__ on TikTok.
